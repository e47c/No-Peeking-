Privacy Policy

App: No peeking!  •  Effective date: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information No peeking! (the "App") uses, and what happens to it. The App is run by The 47th Frame Team ("we", "us"). We keep it simple because we collect very little.

1. Information we use

From your Discord account (when you press "Authorize" in Discord, using the identify permission):

  • your Discord user ID,
  • your username / display name,
  • your avatar picture.

We use these only to show you and the other players in the game, and to know who is who.

What you upload in a game:

  • the images you upload for your cards,
  • the character names you type.

Game information: which voice-channel activity session you are in, your score, and your guesses and votes in the current game.

2. How long we keep it

We do not use a database and we do not save your uploads to disk. Everything above is kept only in the server's temporary memory while the game is running:

  • each round's images are deleted when that round ends;
  • all images are deleted when the game ends or the host returns to the lobby;
  • everything is deleted when the room becomes empty, after at most 2 hours, or when the server restarts.

Your Discord login token is used once to check who you are, and is not stored.

3. Who can see it

  • Other players in your game can see your Discord name and avatar, your score, and the cards (image and name) shown during the game. You never receive your own hidden card until it is revealed.
  • Discord receives the normal requests needed for the Activity to run.
  • Our hosting provider (Render) runs the server and may keep normal technical logs such as IP addresses and request times.

We do not sell your information, do not show ads, do not use analytics or tracking cookies, and do not share your information with anyone else.

4. Children

The App is meant for people who are allowed to use Discord. It is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from them.

5. Your choices

You can leave the game at any time, which removes your uploaded cards from the lobby. Because we do not keep your data after the game, there is nothing stored to delete later. You can also remove the App's access in Discord under User Settings → Authorized Apps. For any question or request, contact us below.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information the App handles, such as never sending a player their own hidden card and never writing images to disk. No online service can be perfectly secure.

7. Changes

If the App changes how it uses information, we will update this page and the date above.
