    Curtain call

      How to play

      1. Upload. Pick 3 pictures of characters (one per round) and write each name.
      2. Cards are dealt. You hold a card someone else made. Everyone sees it except you.
      3. Ask. On your turn, ask yes/no questions out loud. Friends answer, and can tap YES / NO / MAYBE.
      4. Guess. Think you know? Press “I know it!”, say it out loud, and the others vote.

      Faster guesses score more. Three wrong guesses and you sit out the round. 3 rounds. Pictures are deleted after every round.
